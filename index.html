Project 2: E-Commerce Website

A modern, full-featured e‑commerce website designed to deliver a fast, secure, and accessible shopping experience across desktop and mobile. The site showcases a dynamic product catalog with category drill-downs, faceted search and filtering, product detail pages with high-resolution imagery and zoom, customer reviews, related product suggestions, and inventory-aware variants (size, color, stock status). The shopping cart and checkout flow are streamlined and optimized for conversions — guest checkout, saved payment methods, address validation, and clear order summaries reduce friction for buyers. Payments are integrated with industry-standard gateways (Stripe and PayPal) using secure tokenization and HTTPS throughout.

On the technical side the project is built with a modern JavaScript stack: a component-driven frontend (React or Vue) for interactive UI, server-side rendering for SEO and performance, and a robust backend (Node.js/Express or similar) with a relational or document-based database depending on data needs. The architecture emphasizes modularity and observability — APIs are versioned, and critical flows (checkout, inventory updates) are covered by unit and end-to-end tests (Jest, Cypress). For real-world readiness the app includes authentication with secure session handling, role-based admin access for product and order management, and basic customer account features (order history, address book, password reset).

Performance and reliability are central: images and static assets are served via a CDN with responsive image techniques and lazy loading, server responses are cached where appropriate, and the build is optimized to minimize bundle size. Accessibility and internationalization are supported through semantic markup, ARIA attributes, keyboard navigation, and flexible locale/date/currency formatting. Shipping rules, tax calculations, and promotional coupons are configurable; analytics and event tracking are included to measure conversion and guide iterative improvements. The project is containerized (Docker) with a CI/CD pipeline for automated testing and deployment, making it easy to scale and maintain in a production environment.
